CASE 008 · BANGALORE TRIO ARCHIVE

The Pizza, Drinks & iPad Treaty 🍕🎲

Two drinking partners. One non-drinker with a very clear agenda. One enormous Domino’s pizza and an iPad running UNO and Ludo.

THE CAST:

Megha + Pranav: drinking partners 🍻

Me: present for the important things in life: Domino’s BIG BIG PIZZA, UNO, and Ludo on the iPad. 🍕🎮

Different beverages, different priorities, same hangout. Nobody needed identical plans to be part of the same memory.

THE GURGAON UPDATE: Pranav has now left Bangalore for a job at another company. The Bangalore era has changed, but the pizza-and-games archive stays open. ❤️

FINAL VERDICT: Trio bonding does not require matching drink orders. It requires good company, a big pizza, and the occasional argument over UNO. 💀

CASE 009 · PRANAV SURVIVAL UNIT

The McDonald’s-to-Democracy Incident 🍔🇮🇳

A questionable meal schedule, a farewell party, two concerned friends, and one deeply unexpected political question.

EXHIBIT A — THE SURVIVAL PLAN 🍔

Pranav would sometimes go the whole day without eating, then have one McDonald’s meal and call it a day. Megha and I were genuinely chintit about how this man was surviving. 😭

EXHIBIT B — THE FAREWELL PARTY

Before leaving Bangalore, Pranav threw a party. His plan involved not eating all day before drinking and getting high. The plan apparently worked a little too well—and Megha and I ended up sambhal-ing him.

EXHIBIT C — THE WASHROOM PHILOSOPHER 🇮🇳

I got him to the washroom. Instead of doing what he had gone there to do, he looked at me and said:

“Can you believe we live in a democratic country?”

ME: “WHATTTT? BHAI, ABHI KYUN? 💀”

FINAL VERDICT: Pranav moved to Gurgaon. The democracy question remains in Bangalore's unsolved files.

CASE 010 · SUSPECT PROFILE · TOP SECRET

The Expressionless Menace: Dance, Drama & Deception 🎭

A dead-serious face, two extremely believable lies, and a school-time superstar nobody saw coming.

SUBJECT PROFILE: The person his friends call “expressionless”—and the same person who can deliver a joke with the gravity of an official government announcement.

EXHIBIT A — THE ₹5,000 FAREWELL BILL 💸

At a friend's farewell, the bill arrived. I said it was ₹5,000. Megha believed me—until she looked at the bill and discovered it was actually half that. The face stayed serious. The scam collapsed only after a document check. 😭

EXHIBIT B — THE MYSORE BOOKING SCANDAL 🏨

Before Megha had even got permission for our upcoming Mysore trip, I told her I had already booked the stay. She believed me. Reality: the group was going to look for and book the stay together. The trip hadn't even started, and the imaginary booking was already causing trouble. 💀

EXHIBIT C — THE FORGOTTEN SCHOOL SUPERSTAR 🎬

Current reputation: expressionless, serious, impossible to read. Historical record: once the best dancer and actor in school.

Friends remain unconvinced. The archive has no explanation for how the stage performer evolved into a straight-faced misinformation department.

FINAL VERDICT: Subject is not emotionless. Subject is simply impossible to read. All announcements should be verified; all school-stage credentials remain legendary.
